CL Panner

CL Panner is an application designed for Ableton Live that lets you create spatial mixes by modeling the placement of 2 to 12 speakers in a scaled 3D space, giving you greater creative freedom in sound design.

CL Panner communicates with Live via OSC, allowing you to mix on site using one computer running CL Panner and another running Live connected to the playback system.

Designed for macOS

2–12 speakers · 3D spatial positioning · OSC communication
