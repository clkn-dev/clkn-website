Privacy

CL Panner Privacy Policy

CL Panner does not collect, store, track, or transmit personal information to CLKN.

Local Network

CL Panner uses the local network to communicate with compatible software using the OSC protocol. This communication takes place between devices on the user's local network and does not require an Internet connection.

Files

CL Panner may read and write files selected by the user and files used by the application on the user's Mac. These files remain on the user's device and are not transmitted to CLKN.

Subscriptions

CL Panner offers an optional subscription through Apple's App Store. Purchases and subscription management are processed by Apple using StoreKit. CLKN does not receive or store payment information.

Analytics and Tracking

CL Panner does not use advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking technologies.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact: support.clkn@gmail.com
